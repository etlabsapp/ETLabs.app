ETLabs MyHQ: put it online and on your phone

1. What it is

ETLabs MyHQ is a one-page dashboard for your deadlines, this week, to-dos, calendar and links. It runs in your web browser, and your data is saved there, on your device.

The whole site is one folder called public. There's nothing to build or install. You just put that folder online as it is.

2. Put it online

You need a free "static host." That's a service that shows your files as a website. Cloudflare Pages is one.

  1. On your host, pick the option to upload files yourself, often by dragging and dropping.
  2. Drag in the public folder. Don't add, remove or rename anything in it.
  3. Make sure the file named _headers goes up with it. It tells browsers to only load MyHQ's own files and never send anything to other sites. It also keeps the page out of search results and makes sure phones get updates.
  4. When it finishes, the host gives you a link. Save it for your phone.

Uploading a new version? Open sw.js in the public folder and change the number after VERSION near the top first (any new value works, as long as it's different). If you don't, phones that already added MyHQ won't update.

3. Set up your phone

  1. Open the link on your phone.
  2. Add it to your Home Screen.
    • iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
    • Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Add your items. Open MyHQ from your Home Screen. Under Pick a starter, choose the one that fits you: Small Business / Freelancer, Student, Veteran / Career Transition, Job Seeker, Parents, Stay-at-Home Parent or Homeschool. Or tap Start blank. A starter fills in sample items; tap Remove sample items to clear them when you're ready. Then tap + and choose New deadline or New to-do.
  4. Make it yours. Tap Settings. Under Layout, pick List (everything on one page), Week (a Mon–Sun planner) or Cards (every open item as a tile). Under Colors, pick Paper, Harbor, Fern, Clay or Ink. Your choice applies right away. You can also give your page its own name there.

Don't see a format you like? The last card under Pick a starter is Don't see a format you like? Tell us. Describe what you keep track of, tap Suggest a format, then Use this format to start with it.

After your first visit, it works without internet too.

You can add your own deadlines and to-dos.

4. Back up and restore

To back up:

  1. In the Your data box, tap Export backup.
  2. A file named like hq-backup-(date).json saves to your device. Keep it safe.

The page tells you when your last backup was. After 14 days, it reminds you to make a new one.

To restore (for example, on a new phone):

  1. Open your link and tap Restore from file. On a brand-new phone, you'll find it under "Have a backup file?"
  2. Pick your backup file.
  3. If that device already has MyHQ data, it asks before replacing everything. Confirm to go ahead.

5. Our privacy promise

No account, no tracking: what you type stays on your device, and ET Labs never sees it.

Backups save as a file on your device. Delete the app without one, and your data is gone.
