Client work

Built for real businesses.

Client website · Southeastern US

Southeastern Coatings

The business: a family-run asphalt company doing sealcoating, crack filling and line striping for parking lots and driveways in the Southeastern US.

The problem: customers needed a simple way to see the work and ask for an estimate from their phone.

What I built: a fast, mobile-first website with a step-by-step free estimate request that opens a ready-to-send text message, a QR code so desktop visitors can text from their phone, an Our Work page of past jobs, customer reviews, and a sealcoating FAQ.

Disclosure: it's a family-run business, and the owner is my brother-in-law.
