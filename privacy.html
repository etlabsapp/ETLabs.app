Privacy

Privacy — etlabs.app

Last updated: October 27, 2026

ET Labs keeps this simple. The site doesn't use accounts or ads, and it doesn't use tracking cookies.

What we measure

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits on the marketing pages. It doesn't use cookies, and we only see totals, like which pages are viewed and roughly where visitors come from. It doesn't identify you or track you across other sites. The MyHQ app at /myhq/ is not included: those pages do not load the analytics beacon.

Like any host, Cloudflare handles basic request data, such as your IP address, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Total Cross leaderboard

If you add a name to the Total Cross leaderboard, each puzzle you finish after that sends your result to the daily leaderboard: the puzzle, your time, hints used, difficulty, the name you chose, and a random ID the game creates in your browser so it can recognize your device. If you rate a puzzle (easy, tricky, hard or fun), we save that rating with the same random ID, but no name. The random ID isn't linked to your real identity. We store this with our database provider, Supabase. To show the leaderboard, the game loads it from Supabase and loads one code file from the jsDelivr network. Like any host, both see your IP address, even if you never add a name. Please don't use your real name on the leaderboard. To remove an entry, email us.

Saved in your browser

Some pages save things only in your browser, on your device: your MyHQ page, your progress in the Security+ practice games and Total Cross, and whether you've already seen the intro screen. We don't receive any of this. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

MyHQ

Everything you add to MyHQ stays in your browser on your own device. We don't receive it, store it or see it. If you clear your browser data, it's gone.

If you tap Send to tell us which formats you'd like, your own email app opens with a message already filled in with your suggested sections, layout and colors (not text you typed). Nothing goes out unless you send it yourself. If you do, we get that email and use it only to read and reply.

Free QR: codes are made in your browser. What you type isn't sent to us or saved.

Everything else

Apart from the visit totals, the leaderboard entries above and any email you choose to send us, the site collects nothing.

Children

etlabs.app isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has entered personal information, such as a real name on the leaderboard, email us and we'll delete it.
