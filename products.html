Client website · Southeastern US

Southeastern Coatings

The business: a family-run asphalt company that does sealcoating, crack filling, and line striping for parking lots and driveways in the Southeastern US.

The problem: customers needed a simple way to see the work and ask for an estimate from their phone.

What I built: a fast, mobile-first website. A step-by-step free estimate request opens a ready-to-send text message, and a QR code lets desktop visitors send it from their phone. The site also has an Our Work page of past jobs, customer reviews, and a sealcoating FAQ.

Disclosure: Southeastern Coatings is owned by my brother-in-law.
