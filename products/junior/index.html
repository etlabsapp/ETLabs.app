Concept · Coming soon

AI learning for kids and parents

ET Labs Junior is a concept we're building: a place where kids and parents learn about AI together by playing, asking questions and trying things. Here's some of what's planned. None of it is ready yet, so there's nothing to sign up for.

  • Teach the Robot

    Kids show a robot a few examples, watch it learn, and see where it gets mixed up. It's a hands-on way to see that AI learns from what people give it.

  • Story Builder

    Kids tell the AI their idea, and the story grows from there. The idea always starts with the kid.

  • Weekend AI Lessons

    Short weekend lessons that families can do together. Each one explores one AI idea through play.

  • Parent Corner

    A space for parents to see what their kids are learning, with simple ways to talk about AI at home.

For parents: Junior is being designed with kids' safety in mind from the start, and we'll share more here when it's ready.
